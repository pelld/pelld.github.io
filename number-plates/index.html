The registration

AB-123-CD is a national serial number

The SIV number is assigned automatically in a single national sequence. It is not a code for a place, and you cannot choose it. Once assigned, it normally stays with the vehicle for its lifetime.

2009
SIV began for new vehicles on 15 April 2009.
7
Alphanumeric characters: 2 letters, 3 digits, 2 letters.
∞
The number stays with the vehicle through changes of owner or address.

The clever bit: 76 does not mean “from Seine-Maritime”

The territorial identifier is a choice.The owner can choose any French département identifier, whether or not they live there. The regional logo must match that département.

Older French plates worked differently

1234 AB 76

The old FNI system

On older registrations, the number at the end really was tied to the département of registration. Old-format plates can still be seen on vehicles that have not yet had a transaction that moves them into SIV.
